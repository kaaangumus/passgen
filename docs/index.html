Built for Real Security & Productivity

Everything you need to create unbreakable credentials without slowing you down.

Smart Right-Click Auto-Fill

Right-click on any password input and select "Generate Password". It instantly creates a password, fills the input, and copies to your clipboard.

CSPRNG Cryptographic Engine

Zero pseudo-randomness. PassGen utilizes the Web Cryptography API (crypto.getRandomValues) to ensure entropy that withstands quantum-level predictability.

100% Offline & Private

No backend, no databases, no tracking analytics. Passwords never travel across the wire and exist solely in your local browser memory.

Shoulder-Surfing Masking

Toggle mask/unmask on generated passwords and history items with a single click. Safely generate credentials in public or screen-shares.

Recent History Recall

Accidentally closed your registration tab? PassGen stores your last 5-10 generated passwords locally on your machine with one-click copy and clear buttons.

Global Hotkey (Alt+Shift+G)

Generate and copy strong passwords instantly without even clicking the browser toolbar. Keep your hands on the keyboard.
